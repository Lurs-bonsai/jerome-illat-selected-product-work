# Jérôme Illat — selected product work

I build products where business needs, technology and adoption have to meet. These three short case studies cover enterprise product work, an early-stage venture, and a human-governed agentic research prototype.

## Hilo — AML/KYC for legal professionals

**Role: Product Manager.** I shaped the product vision and roadmap and coordinated delivery with legal, compliance, design and engineering specialists. Hilo was deployed across two internal entities. The product was recognised in Chartis Research's Financial Crime and Compliance50 in 2025 and 2026.

[Read the Hilo case study](Jerome%20Illat%20-%20Hilo%20case%20study.pdf) · [Hilo product site](https://hilo-global.com/) · [Chartis 2025](https://www.chartis-research.com/financial-crime/7947309/financial-crime-and-compliance50-2025) · [Chartis 2026](https://www.chartis-research.com/financial-crime/7947472/financial-crime-and-compliance50-fcc50-2026-ranking-and-award-winners)

## Bō — Art of Bonsai

**Role: Founder & CEO.** I built an early-stage venture joining bonsai craft, connected hardware, sensor data, machine learning and a premium service experience. The venture received the Prix de l'Excellence Française in 2018.

[Read the Bō case study](Jerome%20Illat%20-%20Bo%20case%20study.pdf) · [Excellence Française — Jérôme Illat (Bō) 2018](https://excellencefrancaise.com/fr/membres-de-l-excellence-francaise/le-cercle/)

## AI Research — Investment Family Office

**Role: Product lead and agent-system designer.** I orchestrated AI-assisted product delivery with Claude Code, Cursor, specialist agents, reusable skills and quality gates across roughly 40 scoped lots. I also designed an investment research workflow in which a Chief Investment Officer agent coordinates specialist perspectives, preserves risks and disagreement, and returns the final choice to a human. The illustrated case is synthetic; this is a personal prototype, not a live investment service.

[Read the AI Research case study](Jerome%20Illat%20-%20AI%20Research%20Family%20Office%20case%20study.pdf)
